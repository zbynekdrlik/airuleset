### Verify Launched Work Stays Alive — Poll It; a Dead Process Sends No "Done" → on-demand skill `verify-launched-work-liveness`

The full liveness-polling protocol moved VERBATIM to the `verify-launched-work-liveness` skill — load it whenever you launch anything long-running (background Bash, a build, a dispatched subagent/workflow) and end a turn `⏳ WORKING`. Non-negotiable that survives here: NEVER infer "still running" from silence — a dead/OOM-killed process emits no "done" and no "failed", so every wait must also terminate on death/timeout, not just success. A bare `run_in_background` poll does NOT survive context **compaction** (SIGTERMed with no re-invoke) — use a foreground bounded loop or `ScheduleWakeup` with a **PLAIN prompt** (never a slash command).
