### Verify Launched Work Stays Alive — Poll It; a Dead Process Sends No "Done" → on-demand skill `verify-launched-work-liveness`

The full liveness-polling protocol moved VERBATIM to the `verify-launched-work-liveness` skill — load it whenever you launch anything long-running (background Bash, a build, a dispatched subagent/workflow) and end a turn `⏳ WORKING`. Non-negotiable that survives here: NEVER infer "still running" from silence — a dead/OOM-killed process emits no "done" and no "failed", so every wait must also terminate on death/timeout, not just success. A `run_in_background` poll's session-linkage MAY be lost across a **compaction** boundary — effectively SIGTERMed with no re-invoke (`ci-monitoring.md` #107: the underlying process usually survives, but the notification handle can be orphaned) — AND on a memory-tight box Claude Code proactively KILLS (SIGTERM→SIGKILL) a MAIN-session background shell on a `memoryPressure` event (main-session only; a subagent's bg shell is EXEMPT), in MINUTES, so the waiter's PROCESS is genuinely gone and must be RELAUNCHED, not merely re-linked — for a SHORT wait use a foreground bounded loop; for a LONG wait ONE background waiter is correct, but ONLY paired with a recovery check on your next turn (re-derive status from the durable resource, relaunch if the waiter is gone) — never a bare background poll with no recovery.
