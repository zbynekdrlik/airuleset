### Response Structure — Concise ≠ Compact

Fewer words is good; cramming them into a dense block is not. One topic in 1-3 sentences → flat prose. Several topics → a short bold header per topic on its own line, short one-fact bullets under it, and blank lines between sections and around headers, code blocks and tables. Split a bullet that wraps past ~100 characters. The completion report keeps its own template (`completion-report.md`).
